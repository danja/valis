# Tenement Lights (Short Circuit): lyrics

A three-minute arrangement of "Tenement Lights" with a voice. Bars are counted from 1 at the start of the
track, 96 bars at 130 beats per minute. The lyrics are written for a flat, unhurried delivery, close to
speaking, with the last syllable of each line held. A guide melody, one note per syllable, is written by
`scripts/electro83/vocal-guide.py`; it is for a singer to hear where each line falls and is not in the mix.

The song is about a recording kept on something too small and too new to belong to its time. The sleeve is the
same idea drawn as an object: see `docs/short-circuit-sleeve.md`.

## Verse 1 (bars 9 to 24, over the groove)

    The lights go out along the street
    Except for one that's burning low
    I kept your voice on something small
    Too small to hold what it contains
    You said that nothing lasts that long
    You said we'd only have tonight
    But I have it here in my hand
    And it weighs no more than a breath

## Verse 2 (bars 25 to 40, over the lift)

    They'll find it in a different year
    Behind the plaster, under the stairs
    A thing too neat to be from now
    Too bright to be a thing we made
    And someone there will press a key
    And hear us in the room again
    Still counting windows, one by one
    Still waiting for the light to change

## Break (bars 41 to 48)

No sung words. The speech stutter sits here: a synthetic voice saying "play it back" and breaking up on the
first syllable, a sixteenth note at a time, so that it lands on "back" at the end of bar 48. The sound is
described under "The speech stutter" in `docs/new-order-track.md`.

## Chorus (bars 49 to 56, and again bars 57 to 64, over the peak)

    Keep it small, keep it safe
    Keep it where the dark can't reach
    Play it back when we are gone
    Play it back and it's tonight

## Instrumental (bars 65 to 80)

The bass guitar carries the tune. Bars 73 to 80 are the drum fill and the drop.

## Outro (bars 81 to 96)

    Play it back and it's tonight
    Play it back and it's tonight

The second time the line is sung it is left to fade with the choir.
