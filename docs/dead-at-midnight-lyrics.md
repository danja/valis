# Dead at Midnight

140 BPM, half-time feel, E minor. The chords change every two bars: Em, D, C, B.
A bar lasts 1.71 seconds, so a four bar line has room for about a dozen syllables
delivered slowly. Sing or speak low and close to the microphone; let the echo finish
each line.

Times are from the start of the song. Bar numbers count from 1.

---

## Intro (bars 1 to 8, 0:00 to 0:14)

Organ and ghost choir. Theremin enters at bar 5. No words until the last bar, then a
whisper, dry:

> *The bell begins.*

---

## Verse 1 (bars 9 to 24, 0:14 to 0:41)

Spoken, half sung. One line to every four bars.

> The clock in the tower has lost its hands,
> and the moon walks slowly across the land.
> Every window is shuttered tight,
> but something is moving in the churchyard light.

*Guitar answers each line with its echo. The last line leans on the B chord.*

---

## Build (bars 25 to 32, 0:41 to 0:55)

The kick speeds up and the saucer rises. Two short lines, the second whispered over
the snare roll:

> The earth is soft where the old stones lean.
>
> *Count the bells you have never heard.*

---

## Drop 1 (bars 33 to 48, 0:55 to 1:22)

The hook, chanted by one low voice and doubled an octave up on the second pass. One
line to every two bars, so the chord changes under it.

> Dead at midnight, dead at midnight,
> cold hands rising in the pale moonlight.
> Dead at midnight, dead at midnight,
> dance till the morning bites.

*Repeat once, ending on "bites" with the saucer dive on the last beat of bar 48.*

---

## Break (bars 49 to 56, 1:22 to 1:36)

Organ, theremin and ghost choir only. A single voice, no echo, very close:

> I was not afraid of the dark.
> The dark was afraid of me.

---

## Verse 2 (bars 57 to 72, 1:36 to 2:03)

Same delivery as the first verse, a little lower.

> The ravens know what the preacher denied,
> that the ones who sleep were never quite satisfied.
> Count the candles and count them twice,
> there is always one more than the price.

---

## Drop 2 (bars 73 to 88, 2:03 to 2:31)

The hook again. On the second pass change the last line, and let the theremin carry
the melody over the top:

> Dead at midnight, dead at midnight,
> cold hands rising in the pale moonlight.
> Dead at midnight, dead at midnight,
> and you were never leaving tonight.

---

## Outro (bars 89 to 104, 2:31 to 2:58)

The layers fall away. Spoken, fading, one phrase every four bars, the echo left to
repeat after the last word:

> Dead at midnight.
>
> The bell has stopped.
>
> The door is open.
>
> *Goodnight.*
